Custom Elementor widgets, site settings and design system for The Jolly Joiner website.

== What it does ==

* Adds a "Professor Web" category to Elementor with 11 widgets:
  PW Header, PW Footer, PW Home Hero, PW Page Hero, PW Content Block, PW Section Heading,
  PW Card Grid, PW Stats Strip, PW Scrolling Ribbon, PW Call to Action, PW Contact Details.
* Adds a "Professor Web" screen in the dashboard for business details (phone, email, address,
  Facebook), header/footer text, brand colours, wood texture strength and animation settings.
* Bundles Bebas Neue and Lato (no Google Fonts requests) and stops Elementor loading Google Fonts.
* Shortcodes: [pw_phone], [pw_email], [pw_address], [pw_year].

== Editing tips ==

* Headings: words inside {curly brackets} are shown in gold.
* Menus: Appearance → Menus. The "Main Menu" item with sub-items becomes the Services dropdown;
  each card uses the linked page's Featured image and the menu item's Description.
* Header and footer are Elementor Theme Builder templates ("Site Header" / "Site Footer").
* Click-to-call buttons only show on phones and tablets; on desktop the number is plain text.

== Requirements ==

Elementor and Pro Elements (or Elementor Pro) for the header/footer templates and the contact form.
